# 21-08-2026

**SERVICENOW SLA CLOCK PAUSES ON "AWAITING CUSTOMER"**

An incident that looked like a 6-hour SLA breach was actually well within target - ServiceNow pauses the SLA timer whenever a ticket sits in an "awaiting customer/awaiting info" state, and only the "in progress" time counts against the clock.

Meant a genuinely fast resolution looked bad on a dashboard that didn't account for the pause, until I checked the actual business-time field instead of wall-clock elapsed time.
