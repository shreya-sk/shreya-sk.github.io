# 15-06-2026

**READINESS VS LIVENESS PROBES**

Spent an hour convinced a pod was crash-looping when it was actually the probe config. Liveness probe was hitting the same slow-starting endpoint as readiness, with too short an `initialDelaySeconds` - so Kubernetes kept killing a pod that just hadn't finished booting yet, not one that was actually unhealthy.

The mental model that finally stuck: readiness controls traffic (should this pod get requests right now), liveness controls restarts (should this pod be killed and replaced). A slow-starting app almost never needs an aggressive liveness probe.
