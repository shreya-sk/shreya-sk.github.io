**DIAPHRAGMATIC BREATHING & THE HUNDRED**

Watched a client do a technically perfect Hundred with zero core engagement, because she was breathing into her chest the whole time. Chest breathing recruits the accessory neck and shoulder muscles instead of the diaphragm and transverse abdominis - so the "hardest ab exercise in Pilates" was doing almost nothing for her core.

Cued lateral rib breathing instead (ribs expand sideways, not up) and the difference in how much she was shaking by rep 60 was immediate. Breath pattern isn't a detail, it's half the exercise.
