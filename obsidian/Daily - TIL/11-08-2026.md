# 11-08-2026

**ETCD BACKUP FOR CKA PREP**

Practising the etcd backup/restore flow for the CKA exam and it's less forgiving than it looks under time pressure: `etcdctl snapshot save` needs the right `--cacert`/`--cert`/`--key` flags pointed at etcd's own certs (not the cluster API server's), and restoring means standing up a fresh data directory, not overwriting the live one in place.

Timed myself doing it cold: 6 minutes. Exam gives less room for fumbling flag names than that.
