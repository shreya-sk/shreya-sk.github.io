**LATERAL BREATHING VS VERTICAL BREATHING**

Most people default to vertical breathing under exertion - shoulders and chest rising - which is fine for a sprint but works against you in Pilates, where you need the ribcage to expand sideways while the deep core stays engaged the whole time.

Cueing "breathe into your hands" with palms pressed against the sides of the ribs gives people something concrete to feel, rather than just telling them to "breathe laterally," which means nothing to a first-timer.
