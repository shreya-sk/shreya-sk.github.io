**SCAPULAR WINGING CUE**

A client's shoulder blade kept lifting off the ribcage during plank-style work - classic serratus anterior weakness, not a flexibility issue. Cueing "push the floor away" (protraction) rather than just "shoulders down" gets the serratus actually firing, versus a generic cue that just tightens the upper traps instead.

Small wording change, completely different muscle recruited. Cueing anatomy correctly matters as much as programming the right exercise.
