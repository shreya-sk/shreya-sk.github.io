# 22-07-2026

**THORACIC MOBILITY HIDING IN THE LUMBAR SPINE**

A client with "tight lower back" during rollups turned out to have almost no thoracic spine rotation or extension at all - the movement she needed from her mid-back was happening in her lumbar spine instead, which doesn't articulate the same way and takes the strain.

Spent the session on thoracic extension over the box before going back to rollups. The lower back wasn't the problem; it was just the joint absorbing what the thoracic spine wouldn't give up.
