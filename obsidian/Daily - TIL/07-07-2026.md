**MOTOR UNIT RECRUITMENT: SLOW VS FAST**

Why do slow, controlled reps burn out a muscle faster than the same number of quick reps? Slow tempo keeps the muscle under continuous tension, recruiting and fatiguing motor units progressively (smaller, fatigue-resistant ones first, then larger fast-twitch units as the small ones tire) without the brief relaxation phase fast reps give you.

Reformer work leans hard on tempo for exactly this reason - you don't need heavy resistance if time-under-tension is doing the recruiting for you.
