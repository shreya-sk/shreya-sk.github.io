# 11-06-2026

**ANTERIOR PELVIC TILT**

Third client this month with the same pattern: tight hip flexors, weak glutes, low back that "just aches" by 3pm. All three have desk jobs. Not a coincidence - sitting all day shortens the iliopsoas and switches off the glutes reciprocally, and the pelvis tips forward to compensate, which shows up as an exaggerated lumbar curve.

Cueing a posterior pelvic tilt in isolation doesn't stick - it has to come with actually lengthening the hip flexors first, or you're just fighting the tissue. Reminder to myself too: I sit at a desk all day.
