**CLOSED VS OPEN CHAIN AT THE KNEE**

A client with mild knee pain assumed leg extensions (open chain - foot free, moving against resistance) would be gentler than squats (closed chain - foot planted). It's usually the opposite: open-chain knee extension isolates the quad and puts more pure shear force through the patellofemoral joint, while closed-chain work distributes load across the hip, knee and ankle together.

Swapped her leg-extension-style reformer work for footwork in straps instead - same quad activation, far less isolated joint stress.
