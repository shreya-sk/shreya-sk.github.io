**PSOAS: RELEASE IT OR STRENGTHEN IT?**

Ongoing debate in every anatomy forum: tight hip flexors from sitting - stretch them, or strengthen the opposing glutes instead? Landed on "both, in order": a psoas that's been shortened all day doesn't lengthen back out from strength work alone, but strengthening glutes without addressing flexor tightness just adds force on top of a joint that's already pulled out of neutral.

Static stretch first, then glute activation work in the same session, works better than either alone in my experience.
