# 02-09-2026

**BITNAMI'S REGISTRY RESTRUCTURE BROKE A CHART OVERNIGHT**

A Helm chart that worked fine last month suddenly couldn't pull its bundled Postgres/Redis/Minio images. Bitnami restructured their registry in August, and images that used to live under a stable path moved to a `bitnamilegacy` path that isn't mirrored in our internal registry at all - so the chart's "quick install" defaults just silently stopped resolving.

Ended up skipping the bundled images entirely and provisioning each backing service the way we already do elsewhere (CRD-based operators). More setup, but not dependent on a vendor's registry layout holding still.
