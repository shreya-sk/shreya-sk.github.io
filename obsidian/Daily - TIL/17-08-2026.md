# 17-08-2026

**IIS APP POOL RECYCLE EATS SESSION STATE**

A legacy .NET app kept silently logging users out mid-session with no error anywhere. IIS recycles app pools on a schedule by default (memory thresholds, idle timeout, or just a fixed time of day) - and in-process session state doesn't survive a recycle unless it's externalised.

Not something you'd hit on a modern stateless service, but this app predates that pattern entirely. Turned off the scheduled recycle for now; moving session state out-of-process is the actual fix, filed for later.
