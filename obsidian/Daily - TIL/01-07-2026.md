# 01-07-2026

**DOMS ISN'T LACTIC ACID**

Corrected a client (kindly) who blamed "lactic acid buildup" for being sore two days after a reformer class. Lactic acid clears from muscle tissue within about an hour of finishing exercise - delayed onset soreness two days out is from microscopic tears in the muscle fibres, mostly from eccentric (lengthening-under-load) contractions.

Which is exactly what a lot of Pilates is - slow, controlled lowering phases. The soreness is a sign the eccentric work is doing its job, not a sign of a metabolic byproduct sitting around.
