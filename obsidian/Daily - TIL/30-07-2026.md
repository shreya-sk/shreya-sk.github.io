# 30-07-2026

**VAULT LEASES EXPIRE WHETHER YOU RENEW OR NOT**

An app crashed overnight because it fetched a Vault dynamic database credential once at startup and never renewed the lease. The credential's TTL expired at 2am, Postgres rejected the connection, and nothing in the app was watching for that.

Dynamic secrets are a lease, not a one-time fetch - the client is responsible for renewing before TTL or re-fetching entirely. Added a renewal loop; won't be finding out about an expired lease from an on-call page again.
