**TYPE I VS TYPE II FIBRES, PROGRAMMED DIFFERENTLY**

Type I (slow-twitch, fatigue-resistant) fibres respond to high-rep, low-load, longer time-under-tension work; Type II (fast-twitch, powerful, fatigue quickly) need lower reps at higher intensity to actually get trained rather than just tired.

Classic Pilates - high reps, controlled tempo, moderate resistance - is essentially a Type I bias by design. Which is exactly right for postural endurance, but it's not what you'd reach for if the goal is raw power output.
