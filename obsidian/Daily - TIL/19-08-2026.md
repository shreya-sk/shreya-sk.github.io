# 19-08-2026

**TRAINING ZONES, NOT JUST PACE**

Been running training plans off pace alone and plateauing. Heart-rate zones tell a different story: most "easy" runs were actually zone 3-4 effort because pace targets don't account for heat, sleep, or just an off day - the watch doesn't lie about effort the way a pace goal can.

Recalculated zones off a recent max-effort test instead of an age-based formula. Half-marathon block restarts properly easy this time.
