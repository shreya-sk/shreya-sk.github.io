**KONG ROUTE PRIORITY ISN'T DECLARATION ORDER**

Lost twenty minutes assuming Kong Gateway matched routes top-to-bottom like a firewall ruleset. It doesn't - priority is decided by specificity of the path match, not the order routes are declared in the config. A more specific path (`/api/v2/users/:id`) wins over a broader one (`/api/v2/*`) even if the broad one is declared last.

Explains a routing bug that looked completely random until I actually read the specificity rules instead of the file order.
