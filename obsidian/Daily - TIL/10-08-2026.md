# 10-08-2026

**HELM ROLLBACK KEEPS HISTORY**

Nervous about a `helm rollback` deleting the release I was rolling back from. It doesn't - Helm keeps release history by revision number, and a rollback just re-applies an older revision's manifest as a *new* revision on top. Nothing is destroyed; you can roll forward again immediately if the rollback wasn't actually the right call.

Much safer mental model than I'd assumed - it's an insert, not a delete.
