**PROPRIOCEPTION ON AN UNSTABLE SURFACE**

Standing footwork on the reformer with the carriage unlocked (versus locked, on the floor) forces constant small stabilising corrections through the ankle and foot intrinsics that a stable surface never demands - the muscle is working the whole time just to keep the joint where you want it, not just to move it.

Useful progression for anyone post-ankle-injury: the instability itself is the rehab tool, not an obstacle to work around.
